## Video direction

Palette from frame.md (Coral remixed onto brand): cream canvas #FFFCF8, ink #201C19 display/body, Meeko orange #FF5A1F reserved for proof moments — citation chips, VERIFIED/NOT FOUND stamps, the machine's gate. Amber for held warnings. Motion grammar: power3 long-tail eases, VO-paced reveals (nothing appears before the voice names it), subtle jitter at most during holds — no lazy breathing, no screensaver drift. Rhythm: frames 1-2 build tension, frame 3 is the held turn, frame 4 is the long development, frames 5-6 punch, frame 7 lands still. Held frames: 3 (turn) and 7 (lockup). Negative list: no purple-blue AI gradients, no bokeh, no browser chrome, no stock icons standing in for designed metaphor; failure modes banned — slideshow front-load-freeze and screensaver float. Content stays in the top ~83% (caption band clear); centered heroes anchor y ≈ 0.42 × height.
