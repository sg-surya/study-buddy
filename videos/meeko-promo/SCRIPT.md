# Meeko — promo script (verbatim VO, English)

Every student has faced this situation.

It's the night before an exam. Lecture notes are scattered across PDFs, important concepts need revision, and time is running out.

Naturally, students turn to AI for help. They ask questions, get instant answers, and continue studying. But there's a hidden problem.

What if the AI gives an answer that isn't even in their notes?

A confident response can look convincing, but without evidence, students have no easy way to distinguish a grounded answer from a hallucination.

That's where Meeko — Study Companion from Real Notes — comes in.

Meeko transforms ordinary study material into an interactive, evidence-first learning experience.

Students can upload their PDFs, organise their material into notebooks, and ask questions directly from their notes.

Behind the scenes, Meeko processes documents page by page, retrieves relevant passages, and uses Google's Gemma 4 to generate answers grounded in the available evidence.

But the real innovation lies in what happens next.

Meeko doesn't simply generate citations. It verifies them.

Every cited quotation is checked against the original notes using deterministic code. Verified evidence is presented alongside the answer, with page-tagged citations that let students jump directly to the relevant section of their PDF.

And when sufficient evidence isn't available, Meeko can respond with a clear NOT FOUND instead of presenting an unsupported answer as fact.

But learning doesn't stop at asking questions.

Meeko also brings together AI-generated quizzes, interactive flashcards, explanations at different difficulty levels, and audio overviews — all in one study workspace.

Powered by Gemma 4, with local inference through LM Studio, Meeko is designed to make AI-assisted learning more transparent, accessible, and privacy-conscious.

Because the goal isn't simply to help students get answers faster.

It's to help them learn from reliable evidence, understand what they're studying, and know when an answer cannot be verified.
