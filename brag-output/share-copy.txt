Post copy (no emojis, links in first comment):

Built Meeko, a NotebookLM-style study workspace for Hacktoberfest HackDay 2026 (PS-01, Gemma 4).

Drop in notes -> get answers that carry an evidence verdict (SUPPORTED / NOT_FOUND), inline source
chips that jump the built-in PDF viewer to the exact page, an auto briefing with suggested questions,
plus quiz, flashcards and an audio overview.

If the answer is not in your notes, Meeko says NOT_FOUND instead of guessing.

Stack: Gemma 4 (google/gemma-4-e2b) + nomic-embed via OpenAI-compatible router, FastAPI, React,
in-notebook retrieval with chunk/page tracking. Page tags come from the parser, not the model, so
citations stay honest.

Video: 20s, 1920x1080, 30fps, custom score synced to the UI moments.
